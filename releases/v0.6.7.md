# Bestiary Nav 0.6.7

- Levelling selects the nearest eligible enemy of any type inside the patrol circle. The named monster in the area catalog is used to choose a destination, not to restrict targets.
- Preserve the full configured level band when selecting an area, rather than clamping it to one species' documented range. As before, keep this band through level-ups and exclude enemies at or below BST's current level.
- When higher eligible enemies are found within the circle, account for them before marking that area as outleveled. Out-of-range enemies do not extend the stay.
- Preserve FATE selection, Notorious Monster filtering, circle/floor boundaries, and avoidance of enemies already engaged by other players outside FATEs. Capture mode's entry-specific targeting is unchanged.
- Add a **Levelling mode** quick toggle beside **Capture all available** above Master's Bestiary. It uses the same saved settings and start/stop controls as the Levelling tab; the two modes remain mutually exclusive. Show levelling activity and its status tooltip beside the shared Stop button, including while waiting or recovering.

## Validation

Regression checks cover a narrow catalog range inside a wider configured range, mixed-species candidates, and area completion as higher eligible enemies are discovered. Native combat selection still requires an in-game check.
