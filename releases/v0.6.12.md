# Bestiary Nav 0.6.12

- Fixed selected Levelling groups restricting combat to their named monster. Groups now choose patrol destinations; all eligible nearby species are allowed by default.
- Added **Targets in this area** below the monster group selector: choose a zone, search species, and check what to target or uncheck what to ignore.
- Added **Target all** and **Ignore all**, with choices saved separately per zone. Ignore all also excludes newly discovered species until selected.
- Bundled known zone species and level ranges for browsing before travel. Live sightings add other enemies to the list.
- Added **Custom patrol routines**: create a named routine and use **Register checkpoint** at each location. Reorder or remove points, set the checkpoint search radius, and loop the saved route while Levelling. Checkpoints preserve height and use ground paths within one overworld zone.
- Custom routines search for eligible targets at each checkpoint, fight them with Rotation Solver, then continue. Revival retains the current checkpoint; unreachable points are skipped, with a 60-second retry if the whole circuit fails.

Target choices still respect the level range, patrol circle, and FATE/Notorious Monster options. Defense against attackers continues. Editing choices stops Levelling so you can finish selecting before restarting it. Listed species cover the zone and may not currently be present inside the patrol circle.

Selecting a custom routine replaces catalog-group patrols and disables FATE detours. The current level range and per-zone species choices still apply; the routine loops until stopped or the target BST level is reached. Choose **Use catalog patrols** to return to automatic area selection. Editing a routine stops Levelling. Record grounded positions and land before starting a custom ground route.
