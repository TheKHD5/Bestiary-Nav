# Bestiary Nav 0.6.8

- Levelling offsets now follow the current BST level. With minimum and maximum set to +5, BST 30 targets level 35; after reaching BST 31 it targets level 36. An already engaged fight can finish, but unengaged old-range targets are dropped before a new pull.
- Recheck whether the current area supports the updated range. Continue only when documented spawn levels or eligible enemies observed inside the circle support it; otherwise select a suitable destination. Do not silently fall back to weaker targets when no destination is available.
- A completed patrol with no eligible enemies excludes that area for that exact range for the rest of the session. Try other suitable areas instead of repeatedly sweeping the same empty circle. Restarting Levelling clears these exclusions; a different level range has separate search evidence.
- Show the current target range and area's documented levels in settings and Copy diagnostics.
- Detect missing player spells/weaponskills after six ready seconds, independently of the enemy's HP loss. Auto-attacks, pet damage and other players can no longer hide this kind of stalled Rotation Solver combat. Retain the separate eight-second no-damage recovery check.
- Recover with the next learned, unexpired basic combo step instead of always using Smash Axe. Validate the three actions and their combo links against the installed game data; keep game cooldown, queue, capture-mark and target-ownership checks. This applies to Capture, defensive combat and Levelling.
- Keep Rotation Solver's intended external-control (Henched) mode. Include its mode, last confirmed spell/weaponskill age and Levelling's recovery reason in Copy diagnostics. The Henched label itself is not an error; a live stall report is still needed to identify why the solver originally stopped selecting skills.
- Add a shared "Wait for full HP before engaging" setting on the Capture and Levelling tabs. When enabled, new pulls pause until your HP reaches its current maximum, including when HP drops during an approach. Existing fights and defense against attackers continue. Healing does not consume the paused pull/patrol deadline. The default is off, preserving normal Capture behavior and Levelling's existing 70% minimum; no saved settings are changed automatically.

Local regressions cover exact +5/+5 across level-ups, multi-level gains, compatible/incompatible area transitions, mixed-species observations, empty-area exclusions, stalled skills masked by background damage, repeated skill use, recovery pauses, and basic combo progression. Live navigation and combat recovery still need in-game verification.
