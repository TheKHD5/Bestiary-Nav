# Bestiary Nav 0.6.13

- Replaced single-monster dropdown rows with zone categories containing patrol locations and matching monster species.
- Coerthas Central Highlands now lists Snowstorm Goobbue, Bateleur, Ice Sprite, and other known species together when their level ranges overlap your settings.
- Added Target/Ignore checkboxes directly beneath the patrol locations, sharing saved choices with **Targets in this area**.
- Selected-location summaries now show the zone and coordinates. Existing saved patrol selections remain intact.

Species ranges describe the whole zone; a listed species may spawn outside a particular patrol circle or during an event. Actual enemy level, location, and FATE/Notorious Monster rules still determine combat eligibility. Patrol destinations retain their documented level ranges.
